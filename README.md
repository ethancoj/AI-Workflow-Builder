Merchant Visual Workflow Builder for WhatsApp-Native Service Commerce

Final Year Project — Universiti Malaya, Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology

About

A no-code AI workflow builder that lets non-technical merchants design and deploy intelligent WhatsApp chatbot workflows through a visual drag-and-drop interface. Built for small service businesses in Malaysia (aircon servicing, salons, home repair, etc.) that rely on WhatsApp for customer communication.
